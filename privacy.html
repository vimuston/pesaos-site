PesäOS privacy policy

PesäOS is a private home dashboard used only by the members of one household. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data

  • PesäOS asks for access to Google Calendar events only, and only for the household member who signs in.
  • It uses that access to show the member's events and to add, change or delete events when that member asks.
  • The sign-in token is stored encrypted or access-restricted on the household's own home server. Calendar data is kept there and is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • To answer questions, event details may be processed by an AI assistant run on the household's own subscription.
  • A member can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or by disconnecting in PesäOS.

PesäOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
